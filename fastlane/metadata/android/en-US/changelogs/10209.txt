The sidebar is now the one you use on the web: New bot and New chat at the top, Decisions with how many wait, your pinned chats and bots, one Recent list you can narrow to chats or bots, your sections and Projects, and a menu on every row. The app opens where the web does, on Decisions when something waits for you. A rename or a move shows its confirmation, messages sit above the composer, and a wrong password shows on the sign-in form instead of an error screen.
