The sidebar is now the same one you use on the web: New bot and New chat at the top, Decisions with how many wait, your pinned chats and bots, one Recent list you can narrow to chats or bots, your sections and Projects, and a menu on every row to pin, rename, move or delete. The app now opens where the web does, on Decisions when something waits for you. Saving a rename or a move shows its confirmation, and messages sit above the composer instead of over it.
