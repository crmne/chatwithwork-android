Make Chat with Work your phone's digital assistant, and long press the app's icon for New chat, New bot and Decisions. A dictation that couldn't be transcribed is kept, to try again or discard. Every screen shows its shape until its page is there. Tap Chats or Bots again in the sidebar to open its full page. The + menu can narrow a chat's services to some of your accounts or projects, and web search shows as a filled globe when it's on. Answers you leave running show as a Live Update sooner.
